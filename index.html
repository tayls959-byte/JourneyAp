My journal

Your data, your choice

Entries and audio are stored locally in this browser on this device, not in GitHub or ChatGPT. Clearing browser data or changing phones may delete them.

Backups contain sensitive personal information and audio. Keep them somewhere private. Restoring adds entries; matching IDs are replaced.

To save a backup to Google Drive, download it and upload it using Drive. Automatic Drive synchronisation is not enabled.
